## Verify fix: race condition in `renewOnceSafe` (Plan `2026-07-31-1300-4-arm-mr2-fix.md`)

- `TestTakeoverLockHeartbeatRenewal.doExecuteRenewsPeriodicallyAndCancelsOnCompletion` failed with `expected: <9> but was: <10>`
- Root cause: `scheduleWithFixedDelay` + `cancel(false)` race — a renewal tick fires after execution completes because `cancel(false)` does not prevent an already-scheduled task from starting
- Fix: added `runningExecutions.containsKey(sessionId)` guard in `renewOnceSafe` — skip renewal when the session is no longer tracked (execution already finished)
