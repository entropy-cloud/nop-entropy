- **Phase 2 — DbMfaChallengeStore/DbSmsCodeStore**（`nop-auth-service/.../mfa/store/`）：基于 `IDaoProvider`（CRUD `saveEntityDirectly`/`getEntityById`/`updateEntityDirectly`）+ `IJdbcTemplate.executeUpdate`（原子 SQL）。consume 用条件 `DELETE WHERE pk=?
